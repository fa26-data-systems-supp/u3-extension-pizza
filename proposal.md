## 2. Data source
The Museum of Modern Art (MoMA) collected the original data by cataloging works in its collection. For each work, the museum records details such as the title, artist, creation date, medium, dimensions, and acquisition date. MoMA explains that “these datasets are generated from our internal database”. 
- Permission
It released the artwork and artist datasets under CC0 1.0, which allows us to use the data for this project.
